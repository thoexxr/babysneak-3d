隐私政策

别吵醒宝宝 3D（Don't Wake the Baby 3D）· 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 17 日

本隐私政策适用于 iOS 游戏《别吵醒宝宝 3D》（Bundle ID：com.xingranxu.babysneak）。我们尽量把需要的数据降到最低：游戏本体不收集、不上传任何个人数据。

1. 我们收集什么

不收集任何个人数据。游戏没有账号系统、没有广告 SDK、没有第三方分析 SDK、没有内购。

2. 保存在你设备上的数据

  • 关卡解锁进度、最高分、成就解锁状态
  • 音量、亮度、语言、难度等设置
  • 最近一次「保存存档」的进度

以上数据仅保存在本机应用沙盒内，不会上传到我们的服务器。删除 App 即删除这些数据。

3. Game Center

如果你在 iOS 设置中登录 Game Center，游戏会把「已通关关卡数」「无限模式最高分」和成就进度提交给 Apple 的 Game Center 服务，用于排行榜和成就展示。这部分数据由 Apple 处理，适用 Apple 隐私政策。你可以随时在系统设置中退出 Game Center；退出后游戏仍可正常游玩，只是不会提交排行榜和成就。

4. 儿童隐私

本游戏不收集任何用户（包括儿童）的个人信息，也不含广告或外部链接诱导。

5. 数据共享与追踪

我们不出售、不出租、不共享任何数据，也不进行跨 App 或跨网站追踪。App 不请求 ATT 追踪权限。

6. 安全与保留

由于不存在服务器端存储，没有可被我们保留的个人数据。本机存档由 iOS 应用沙盒保护。

7. 政策变更

如果未来加入需要收集数据的功能（例如云存档），我们会在本页面更新政策，并在 App 内说明。

Privacy Policy

Don't Wake the Baby 3D · Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy applies to the iOS game “Don't Wake the Baby 3D” (Bundle ID: com.xingranxu.babysneak). We keep data collection to a minimum: the game itself does not collect or upload any personal data.

1. What we collect

Nothing. There is no account system, no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics SDK and no in-app purchases.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Level unlocks, best scores and achievement progress
  • Volume, brightness, language and difficulty settings
  • The most recent manual save

This data stays inside the app sandbox on your device and is not uploaded to any server we operate. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings, the game submits your cleared-level count, endless-mode high score and achievement progress to Apple's Game Center service for leaderboards and achievements. Apple processes that data under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center at any time; the game remains fully playable, but scores and achievements will not be submitted.

4. Children's privacy

The game does not collect personal information from any user, including children, and contains no ads or external links designed to lure users.

5. Sharing and tracking

We do not sell, rent or share any data, and we do not track you across apps or websites. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

6. Security and retention

Because there is no server-side storage, there is no personal data for us to retain. Local saves are protected by the iOS app sandbox.

7. Changes

If we add a feature that requires data collection (for example cloud saves), we will update this page and explain it in the app.
