Don't Wake the Baby 3D · 技术支持 / Support

这是一款第一人称潜行小游戏：在深夜的家里完成任务，同时别吵醒婴儿床里的宝宝。

常见问题

没有声音？
请检查设备侧面的静音开关、系统音量，以及游戏内「设置 → 音量」。游戏音效在进入关卡后播放。

怎么移动和转视角？
左下角摇杆按住即可移动，右侧滑动转视角；轻推摇杆是踮脚（更安静），推满是快走（更容易吵醒宝宝）。

宝宝多久翻一次身？
大约每 10 秒一次。翻身前 1 秒会出现橙色预警，翻身那 1 秒请原地别动，否则宝宝会睁眼发现你。

进度会丢吗？
关卡解锁、设置和最高分会保存在本机。删除 App 会连同存档一起删除。支持「读取存档」恢复最近一次保存的进度。

Game Center 排行榜/成就打不开？
请先在 iOS「设置 → Game Center」登录 Apple 账户，并确认网络可用。排行榜与成就条目需要 App Store 审核通过后才会显示完整数据。

Support

Don't Wake the Baby 3D is a first-person stealth game: finish chores in a dark house without waking the baby.

Common questions

No sound? Check the silent switch, system volume and the in-game volume setting. Sound effects start once you enter a level.

Controls? Hold the left joystick to move, swipe on the right side to look. A gentle push sneaks; a full push walks and is much louder.

Baby turning? Roughly every 10 seconds. A one-second orange warning appears first — stand still during the turn or the baby will see you.

Progress? Level unlocks, settings and best scores are stored on this device. Deleting the app deletes the save. Use “Load Save” to restore the latest save.

Game Center? Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings and check your network. Leaderboards and achievements become fully visible after App Review.

Purchases/refunds? There are no in-app purchases. For App Store purchase issues use reportaproblem.apple.com.
